## linodectl domains get

List domains

### Synopsis

This is a collection of Domains that you have registered in Linode's DNS Manager. Linode is not a registrar, and in order for these to work you must own the domains and point your registrar at Linode's nameservers. **Permissions and scopes** To call this operation, you need permissions, based on the model you're using: - **Identity and access permissions**. Your user needs a role with these permissions. [Learn more](https://techdocs.akamai.com/cloud-computing/docs/identity-access-cm-available-roles). - Roles: `domain_viewer` - **OAuth scopes**. Your user needs these scopes assigned. [Learn more](https://techdocs.akamai.com/linode-api/reference/get-started#oauth). - Scopes: `domains:read_only` **CLI** ```shell linode-cli domains list ``` [Learn more](https://techdocs.akamai.com/cloud-computing/docs/getting-started-with-the-linode-cli)
