## linodectl service-transfers delete-service-transfer

Cancel a service transfer

### Synopsis

Cancels the service transfer for the provided `token`. These points apply to canceling a service transfer: - An cancellation notification is sent to the email address that's assigned to the account that requested the transfer. - Once canceled, the transfer can't be accepted or acted on in any way. If you cancel by mistake, you need to [create](https://techdocs.akamai.com/linode-api/reference/post-service-transfer) a new service transfer. - You can't cancel a service transfer if it's expired or it's been [accepted](https://techdocs.akamai.com/linode-api/reference/post-accept-service-transfer). **Permissions and scopes** To call this operation, you need permissions, based on the model you're using: - **Identity and access permissions**. Your user needs a role with these permissions.
