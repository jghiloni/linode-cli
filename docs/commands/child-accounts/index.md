### Synopsis

Child accounts let you, as an Akamai partner (a parent account holder), switch between and manage your end customers' accounts (child accounts). Talk to your account team about [setting up a parent-child account relationship](https://techdocs.akamai.com/cloud-computing/docs/parent-and-child-accounts-for-akamai-partners). Once in place, you can use these operations to review your child accounts and create tokens for your use as a parent, on a child account.
