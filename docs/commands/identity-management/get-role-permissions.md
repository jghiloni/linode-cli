## linodectl identity-management get-role-permissions

List available roles

### Synopsis

Lists available account access and entity access roles, their descriptions, and included permissions. Use these roles to manage users' access level to specific services and entities. - **Account access roles**. These roles give access to account level operations, entities in the account both existing and to be created in the future, and creation of new entities. - **Entity access roles**. These roles provide access to specific entities that are already available on the account.
