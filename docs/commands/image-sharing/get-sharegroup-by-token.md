## linodectl image-sharing get-sharegroup-by-token

Get a token's share group

### Synopsis

Gets details about a share group you're a member of. To get the `token_uuid`, run the [Get a token](https://techdocs.akamai.com/linode-api/reference/get-sharegroup-token) or [Create a token](https://techdocs.akamai.com/linode-api/reference/post-sharegroup-tokens) for the share group you want to join and share it with the group owner. You have to be accepted as a share group member before running this operation. **Permissions and scopes** To call this operation, you need permissions, based on the model you're using: - **OAuth scopes**.
