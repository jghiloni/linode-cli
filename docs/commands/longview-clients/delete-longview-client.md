## linodectl longview-clients delete-longview-client

Delete a Longview client

### Synopsis

Deletes a Longview Client from your Account. __All information stored for this client will be lost.__ This _does not_ uninstall the Longview Client application for your Linode - you must do that manually. **Permissions and scopes** To call this operation, you need permissions, based on the model you're using: - **Identity and access permissions**. Your user needs a role with these permissions. [Learn more](https://techdocs.akamai.com/cloud-computing/docs/identity-access-cm-available-roles). - Roles: `longview_admin` - **OAuth scopes**. Your user needs these scopes assigned. [Learn more](https://techdocs.akamai.com/linode-api/reference/get-started#oauth). - Scopes: `longview:read_write` **CLI** ```shell linode-cli longview delete 789 ``` [Learn more](https://techdocs.akamai.com/cloud-computing/docs/getting-started-with-the-linode-cli)
