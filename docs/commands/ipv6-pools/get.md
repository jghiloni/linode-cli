## linodectl ipv6-pools get

List IPv6 pools

### Synopsis

Displays the IPv6 pools on your Account. A pool of IPv6 addresses are routed to all of your Linodes in a single [region](https://techdocs.akamai.com/linode-api/reference/get-regions). Any Linode on your Account may bring up any address in this pool at any time, with no external configuration required. **Permissions and scopes** To call this operation, you need permissions, based on the model you're using: - **OAuth scopes**. Your user needs these scopes assigned.
