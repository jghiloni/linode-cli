## linodectl trusted-devices delete-trusted-device

Revoke a trusted device

### Synopsis

Revoke an active trusted device for your user. Once a trusted device is revoked, this device will have to log in again before accessing your account. **Permissions and scopes** To call this operation, you need permissions, based on the model you're using: - **Identity and access permissions**. Your user needs a role with these permissions. [Learn more](https://techdocs.akamai.com/cloud-computing/docs/identity-access-cm-available-roles). - Permissions: `revoke_profile_device` - **OAuth scopes**. Your user needs these scopes assigned.
